## Overview

The two-dimensional instance of the `GFFPropagator` typeclass. The radial profile
`Cprofile` of the free covariance at $d = 2$ is the modified Bessel kernel
$$C(r) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\,K_0(mr),$$
and its identification with the generic proper-time (Schwinger/heat-kernel) integral
`properTimeCovariance` — the `schwinger_eq` obligation of the typeclass — is proved here. At
$d = 2$ the heat-kernel prefactor is $(4\pi t)^{-1}$, so after pulling out the constant
$(4\pi)^{-1}$ the proper-time integral is exactly the Schwinger integral
$$\int_0^\infty \frac{1}{t}\,e^{-m^2 t - r^2/(4t)}\,dt = 2\,K_0(mr)$$
evaluated in [`General/BesselK0.lean`](../General/BesselK0.md) (`schwingerIntegral_eq_besselK0`),
and $\tfrac{1}{4\pi}\cdot 2 = \tfrac{1}{2\pi}$ closes the computation. The file supplies the
`Fact (2 ≤ 2)` and `Fact (2 ≤ 5)` instances, the `GFFPropagator 2 m` instance, and the shorthand
`μ_GFF2` for the two-dimensional free GFF measure. (The ultraviolet divergence
$C(x_0, x) \to +\infty$ as $x \to x_0$ — logarithmic at $d = 2$ — is the dimension-generic
`freeCovariance_tendsto_atTop` in `OS/NonTrivial.lean`, which specialises to $d = 2$.)
