## Overview

Defines the Euclidean group $E(d) = \mathbb{R}^d \rtimes O(d)$ of a general spacetime
dimension `d : ℕ` and its action $g \cdot x = R(x) + t$ on spacetime, then establishes
the induced pullback actions $(g \cdot f)(x) = f(g^{-1} \cdot x)$ on Schwartz test
functions and on $L^2$ functions. Key supporting results include measure preservation
of each Euclidean motion and polynomial/temperate-growth bounds for the pullback map,
which are needed to keep pullbacks inside the Schwartz space. This is the foundation
for the OS2 Euclidean invariance axiom. The shorthand `E4 := E STDimension` names the
four-dimensional Euclidean group.
