## Overview

Supplies the operations and linearity facts for the complexified test-function
space `TestFunctionℂ d` — the Schwartz functions $\mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{C}$
defined in `Spacetime/Basic.lean` (whose `d = STDimension = 4` shorthand
`TestFunctionℂ4` also lives there). Everything here is dimension-generic, working
over the section variable `{d : ℕ}`. Three groups of results:

- **Complex-linearity of the pairing.** Pointwise real/imaginary arithmetic of
  complex linear combinations is lifted through the real–imaginary decomposition
  `complex_testfunction_decompose` to show that the $\mathbb{C}$-valued pairing
  `distributionPairingℂ_real ω` is $\mathbb{C}$-linear in the test-function
  argument (`pairing_linear_combo`), the key input for bilinearity of the
  Schwinger functions.
- **The real→complex embedding** `toComplex` / `toComplexCLM`: coercing values via
  $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ sends a real Schwartz function to a complex one; the
  embedding is an isometry, so all Schwartz seminorm bounds are preserved, and it
  is packaged as a continuous $\mathbb{R}$-linear map. It intertwines the real and
  complex pairings and generating functionals.
- **Pointwise conjugation** `conjSchwartz`: $f \mapsto \overline{f}$ on complex
  Schwartz functions, again an isometric $\mathbb{R}$-linear operation, with the
  identity $\overline{\langle\omega, f\rangle_{\mathbb{C}}} = \langle\omega,\overline{f}\rangle_{\mathbb{C}}$
  for real distributions.
