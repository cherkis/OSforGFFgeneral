## Overview

Establishes integrability and linear-in-time decay bounds for Schwartz test functions
that vanish on the nonpositive-time half-space, as needed for the OS3 reflection
positivity argument, over a generic dimension `{d : ℕ}` with `[Fact (2 ≤ d)]`.
The key steps are: (1) a Mean Value Theorem bound $\lVert f(x)\rVert \leq C x_0$
for functions vanishing at $t = 0$; (2) a combined FTC + Schwartz-decay bound
$\lVert f(t, x_{\mathrm{sp}})\rVert \leq C t / (1 + \lVert x_{\mathrm{sp}}\rVert)^d$;
and (3) the main linear bound $G(t) \leq Ct$ for the spatial norm integral
$G(t) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^{d-1}} \lVert f(t, x)\rVert\,dx$. Supporting machinery includes
the time-space construction `spacetimeOfTimeSpace` $(t, x) \mapsto (t, x_1, \ldots, x_{d-1})$
with its coordinate, decomposition, and norm lemmas, and integrability of the polynomial
decay $(1 + \lVert x\rVert)^{-d}$ on the spatial slice $\mathbb{R}^{d-1}$.
