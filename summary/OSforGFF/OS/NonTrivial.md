## Overview

Establishes nontriviality / non-degeneracy of the Gaussian Free Field, closing the loophole
that the OS-axiom verification in `OS.Master` would be satisfied trivially by the Dirac measure
at $\omega = 0$ (the "zero field"). Most of the file is dimension-generic: it works over section
variables `{d : ℕ} [Fact (2 ≤ d)]` with `(m : ℝ) [Fact (0 < m)] [GFFPropagator d m]`, proving,
for every dimension:

1. the square-root propagator embedding $T : S(\mathbb{R}^d) \to L^2$ is injective;
2. the smeared covariance $C(f,f) > 0$ for every nonzero test function $f$;
3. every field pairing $\langle\omega,f\rangle$ has strictly positive variance under the free GFF;
4. the GFF measure is not a Dirac measure ($\mu_{\mathrm{GFF}} \neq \delta_0$).

The injectivity chain is: the Fourier transform is injective on Schwartz space (mathlib's
`FourierPair` left inverse $\mathcal{F}^{-1}\circ\mathcal{F} = \mathrm{id}$), the positive
momentum weight $1/\sqrt{\lVert k\rVert^2 + m^2}$ creates no new zeros, and a continuous function
vanishing a.e. (Lebesgue) vanishes everywhere (`volume` is `IsOpenPosMeasure`).

The final section is genuinely **four-dimensional**: `freeCovariance_tendsto_atTop` proves the
UV divergence $C(x,y) \to +\infty$ at coincident points for the 4D Bessel closed form
`freeCovarianceBessel` ($C = \tfrac{m}{4\pi^2 r}K_1(mr)$), driven by the $K_1$ short-distance
asymptotics. It imports `Instances.Dim4Bessel` and is stated over `SpaceTime4`; its analytic
input `besselK1_tendsto_atTop_at_zero` ($K_1(z) \to +\infty$ as $z \to 0^+$) is a real-analysis
lemma living in this same UV section.
